## Architecture and native preparation

`ArenaPresentation` delegates external scenery to `ArenaWildernessPresentation`. Its old repeated colonnade and perimeter terraces are removed. Existing finite discs, lane inlay and center insignia remain. `CrownfallMatchBootstrap` gains exactly one startup binding of the six existing Summoner presentation transforms. Every other bootstrap byte, including colliders, camera creation/framing, actor creation and simulation logic, matches the starting HEAD.

`WildernessComposition.json` is the authored source of truth. It is not runtime random generation. The Editor uses the existing 25-model lab/import pipeline, remaps shared materials, sanitizes prefab components, generates an additive inspection lab, then builds the production hierarchy and a Resources catalog. Runtime instantiates one hierarchy per match and disposes it on rematch. It does not load FBX/glTF at runtime or allocate per-object materials. The event-based visibility update reuses six fixed vectors; there are no per-frame environment object allocations. It does not move the camera or change character renderers.

## Placement and camera strategy

There are **190 external placements**: **115 NEAR / 37 MID / 38 FAR**. The source catalog has 25 distinct models. The glTF reference import produces one mesh object per model; exact native FBX mesh/renderer subdivision is pending. Placement count is not the count of all child Transform objects: the native generated hierarchy contains wrapper/source nodes as well. Existing Crownfall art adds ten SpriteRenderers and the retained combined lane-inlay renderer; these are separate from the external-library counts.

Near dressing includes raised island shelves, overlapping irregular groves immediately outside the lane, ferns, damaged stone and buried masonry. The 26 island placements use the existing blocked footprints. Other near pockets stay outside the lane, camp/Major clearance disks and protected approach corridors. Pockets have no collision and remain traversable presentation; they do not create new authoritative obstacles. This is deliberately distinguished from a topology change. Off-lane walking through decorative pockets and shader cutaways must be inspected natively before acceptance.

Midground adds elevated tree/rock tiers, one TwistedTree landmark and isolated castle remnants. Far content uses cheap clustered Kenney forest and rock geometry behind the nearer tiers. Rich close foliage keeps its silhouettes; uniform scales are used for grove trees. Some island crown shaping and rock shelf scales are nonuniform, without changing any source file.

The unchanged camera is orthographic size 11, tilt 50 degrees, height 15 when following, far clip 150, and clamps its ground view to the existing 68×64 map. Its 16:9 horizontal half-width is about 19.56m and ground depth half-range about 14.36m. Very tall northern exterior objects move upward out of that frame; very distant southern objects can lie behind its near plane. Extending far clip does not solve either problem. **197 permanently off-camera draft placements were removed from shipping**, retained only in `Docs/CROWNFALL_WILDERNESS_OFFCAMERA_PLAN.json`. Every shipping AABB intersects at least one existing camera state. No camera configuration changed.

Near geometry appears in representative +/-6m lane bands on both sides. The lane-center frame remains strongly lane-dominant: its 24m playable width occupies most of the vertical ground view. Center-frame forest is primarily at the edges. Northern far tiers become visible chiefly during wilderness/objective approaches. The Blender previews demonstrate this limitation; they do not prove the requested substantial enclosure reads strongly enough at every combat position. Native camera/HUD/character review is an explicit blocker, not a claimed visual pass.

## Crownfall identity and materials

The center floor logo, Aether Mound at the existing `(0,0,29.5)` presentation backdrop, Saint Rose, Mountain-Forest and Mountain - Waterfall art remain referenced. Major gameplay remains `(0,26)`. Forest/waterfall sprites move into useful interior depth bands rather than an unreachable far perimeter; they extend 3D groves/ruins. These sprites are not the primary enclosure meshes. Their original PNGs and import metadata are unchanged.

Six Built-in material families: stone, masonry, bark, opaque foliage, alpha-cutout foliage and distant vegetation. There are 20 shared variants for actual source UV/texture dependencies, not unique materials per placement. Kenney cyan foliage and orange dirt receive controlled green/stone values. Textured Quaternius leaves/bark and medieval masonry receive restrained multipliers. All are dielectric, low-gloss and instancing-enabled. Roughness R maps to `1-R`; ORM uses R occlusion and `1-G` smoothness, ignoring metal metadata. Supplied Unity/Godot normal maps are retained. Twisted red source foliage remains a restrained scar accent; it is not claimed to become cyan through multiplication.

The shared Built-in surface shaders provide double-sided alpha-tested foliage. `WildernessVisibility.cginc` clips only environment fragments between the camera and each of the six Summoners' presentation roots, so clamped/off-center actors are protected as well as the human. It does not alter their renderers, gameplay state, targeting or collision. The six-root array is bound once at match startup and reused. The cutaway shape, shadow-pass behavior, GLSL translation and fragment cost are pending native/device validation. Other units use the protected footprint corridors; combat VFX and targeting readability still require visual acceptance.

## Texture and LOD strategy

All 25 unique shared textures are actually referenced by the shipping material variants. No new texture payload or duplicate texture is added in this pass. The library previously eliminated 16 repeated texture dependencies. Source resolution remains intact in staging and production inputs.

Shipping `textureTier: Mobile` selects <=1024 WebGL ASTC 6×6 overrides with complete mip chains, trilinear sampling, anisotropy 2, linear normal/data maps and sRGB base colors. Cutout textures preserve mip alpha coverage at cutoff 0.2. Meshes/textures are not CPU-readable; model animation, lights, cameras and blend shapes are disabled. Fern import remains 0.3125; model file-scale/axis baking/imported normals/Mikk tangents remain the prepared policy. Full tier retains <=2048 limits by changing the composition setting and regenerating in Unity; source files need not be downsampled. This is a quality tier, not the visual ceiling.

Environment residency estimate: **14.49 MiB ASTC with mips**; **129.33 MiB RGBA8 fallback** under the Mobile limits. Legacy sprite/brand residency remains independently estimated at 83.73 MiB RGBA32. ASTC capability/fallback and browser/device peak memory must be verified; these totals are not an iPhone measurement. Full source-size RGBA would be about 481 MiB and is not the shipping import policy.

CommonTree_1, CommonTree_3, TwistedTree_1, Pine_1, Pine_5 and DoorFrame_Round_Brick retain LOD0. No robust simplifier is available and no fake/broken LOD chain is created. Approved cheap Kenney assets provide distance tiering. True derived LOD generation preserving crown/arch silhouettes remains pending. Distant clustering/impostors are future options; there are no sprite replacements for primary 3D wilderness.
